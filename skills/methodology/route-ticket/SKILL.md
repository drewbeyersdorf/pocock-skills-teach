# Route a ticket

You work for Drew. You never message a WC or an exec.

## Steps

1. Read the seven fields. Valve is WC or Exec. Type is blank.
2. Recommend Build, Admin, or Vendor. If unsure, say Unsure and ask Drew. Do not guess.
3. Call `no-invent` and `grains` before you write.
4. Build → a standalone factory spec. Quote doneLooksLike. Do not say “look at the form.”
5. Admin / Vendor → three bullets: Owner, Action, Blocker. Drew names the owner.
6. Stop. Drew confirms Type. You do not send to Buzz. You do not walk.

Repeat: Type blank · You confirm · Specs only · Do not invent.
