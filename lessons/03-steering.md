# 3. Words that steer

Agents repeat the dense phrases you put in the skill. Those are **leading words**.

Say “vertical slice” in the skill and you will see it in the trace. Say “Type blank · You confirm · Specs only” and the draft will say it back.

The other steering trick is **legwork**. If a step is fat, split it. Grill-with-docs is its own skill so the agent cannot skip the interview and jump to the spec.

**Try:** write three leading words for your shop. Delete any sentence the agent can ignore without changing the output.
